How Maze Shift works
  1. Rotate the spare maze tile, then push it into one of the marked rows or columns.
  2. The entire line moves and the tile pushed out becomes the next spare tile.
  3. Move your pawn anywhere connected by an unbroken path, or stay where you are.
  4. Land on your current relic to collect it and reveal your next target.
  5. Collect every relic, then return to your coloured starting corner to win.
