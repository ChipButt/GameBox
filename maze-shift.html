Maze Shift

Move the maze. Find your relics. Get back home first.

Who’s playing?

Choose 2–4 players from your GameBox player list.

Game length

How to play
  1. Rotate the spare maze tile, then push it into one of the marked rows or columns.
  2. The whole line shifts. The tile pushed out becomes the next spare tile.
  3. Move your pawn anywhere connected by an unbroken path, or stay where you are.
  4. Land on your current relic to collect it and reveal your next one.
  5. Collect all your relics, then return to your coloured home corner to win.
