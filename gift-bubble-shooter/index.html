HOW TO PLAY

Ready, Aim, Match!

1Drag to aim and release, or tap a target, to launch an ornament. Bank shots off the walls.

2Connect three or more ornaments of the same colour to pop them.

3Loose ornaments fall away. Drop every gift to finish the level.

4Miss too often and a new row arrives. Don't let the stack reach the snow line.

Keyboard: ← → aim · Space shoot · P pause
