The free pack contains Big Silver Pipes (16x16 grid) and Cables (8x8 grid).
The pump.png and broken-pipe.png files used here are small crops/assemblies from the free
Big Pipes SILVER spritesheet, with provenance retained from the public CC0 implementation
in Dungeon-CampusMinden/Dungeon. They are used as the inlet hardware and environmental
pipe detail in Pipeworks.

KARSIORI credit is not required by CC0, but is retained here as a courtesy.
