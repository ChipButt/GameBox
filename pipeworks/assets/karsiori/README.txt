Pipeworks uses the free Big Silver Pipes material from the pack.

pump.png and broken-pipe.png are preserved Karsiori-derived source crops already used by Pipeworks.
The files in tiles/ are 16x16 gameplay modules assembled from the actual free-pack silver pipe and coupling pixels contained in pump.png. They are not SVG redraws. The original pixel colours, shading and edge treatment are preserved while the pieces are recomposed into the five logical puzzle shapes required by the game: end, straight, corner, tee and cross.

All tile PNGs are true RGBA files with alpha transparency.
