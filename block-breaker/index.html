How to Play

Move the paddleDrag or move your pointer across the playfield. Keyboard: ← → or A / D.

Launch the ballTap the playfield or press Space. Keep the ball in play and clear every breakable block.

Know the blocksThick ice takes two hits and visibly cracks after the first. Dark ice cannot be broken. Explosive ice blasts nearby blocks. Special ice always drops a power-up.

Catch the upgradesPower-ups fall toward the paddle. Build combos, survive all 12 Days of Christmas, and chase the high score.
