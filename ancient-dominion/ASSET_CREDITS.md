## Import pipeline

The `tools/import_art.py` script downloads only these named packs from their original OpenGameArt pages and creates local runtime PNG assets. No outside-site runtime hotlinks, purchased assets or commercial game art are required. `assets/manifest.json` preserves exact original download URLs and processed sizes.

This attribution/licensing statement applies to the respective third-party graphic assets; it does not claim that the project's independently authored code or other unrelated files are subject to CC BY-SA.
