Ancient Dominion

Build a civilisation. Command an empire.

A mobile, classic-inspired real-time strategy skirmish. Gather food, wood, stone and gold. Raise an army and destroy the rival village centre.

  • Tap your blue villager, then tap trees, berries or rocks to gather.
  • Drag the land to move the camera; pinch or use +/− to zoom.
  • Select your village centre to train villagers. Select a villager and choose Build to construct buildings.
  • Train troops at the barracks. Tap a troop, then tap where it should move or attack.
  • The mini-map moves your view. Your progress is automatically saved.
